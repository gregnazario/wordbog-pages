How to play

Drag across adjacent dice to spell words before the clock runs out.

Building words

  • Drag across adjacent dice to spell a word — lift your finger to enter it (or tap Submit).
  • Dice connect in any direction, including diagonals. Each die can be used once per word.
  • “Qu” is one cube: drag it once for Q + U.
  • 3+ letters on the 4×4 board, 4+ letters on the 5×5 board — and a real dictionary word.
GAMS ELOT BRIN DCUQu

G → A → M → S along the top row — 5 letters, 2 points.

Scoring

Longer words are worth exponentially more — hunt the 8-letter giants.

3 letters 1 4 1 5 2 6 3 7 5 8+ 11

The 5×5 “Big Rally” board scores the same way but words must be 4+ letters.

Round formats

  • 4×4 Classic and 5×5 Big Rally boards, using the official die distributions.
  • Timed rounds of 1:30, 3:00, or 5:00 — or untimed practice.
  • After every round, see the full solution: your score vs. the maximum, the longest possible word, and every word you missed, traced on the board.
  • Save any board and replay it later from the Saved tab; your games live in History.

Daily Rally

  • Everyone plays the same board every day — one scored 3-minute attempt.
  • Streaks count consecutive days played. Compare scores on Game Center standings.
  • After your scored attempt, the board unlocks as unscored practice with the full solution.

Play a friend

  • Nearby: pair with another device over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth — same board, same clock, live scores, head-to-head results and rematch.
  • Online: Play Online uses Game Center to match you with anyone, anywhere.

Solve a real board

Use Solve Photo to photograph any real (or on-screen) letter-dice board. Crop to the dice, fix any letters the OCR misreads, and Word Rally finds every word on the board. Photos never leave your device.

The dictionary

Words are judged against SOWPODS / Collins Scrabble Words (CSW) — the list competitive play uses — or the smaller Common list for everyday words. Switch between them in Settings; each game is judged by the dictionary it started with.
