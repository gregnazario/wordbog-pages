WordBog

Find the words before the clock runs out.

WordBog is a word-board trainer for iPhone and iPad: timed 4×4 and 5×5 rounds on the official die distributions, a Daily Bog challenge everyone plays on the same board each day, head-to-head play with a friend over the local network or Game Center, and a photo solver that reads a real board from a picture and finds every word.

Everything runs on your device. No accounts, no ads, no tracking, and nothing is collected — see the privacy policy.
